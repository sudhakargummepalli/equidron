Refund and return policy

When you can return something

Each product page shows its return window. The item must be unused, with its tags and invoice, in the condition it arrived. Some items — perishables, innerwear, personalised goods — cannot be returned; their pages say so.

How to ask

Open the order in the store and choose Request return. A courier collects the item, usually within two days.

When the money comes back

The refund goes to the original payment method once we have the item. A gateway refund usually shows within five working days; cash-on-delivery orders are refunded to a bank account you give us.

If we are wrong

Wrong, damaged or missing item: tell us within 48 hours of delivery and we refund or replace it, including the delivery charge.

If a return is refused

We say why, and you can reply. If it cannot be settled, [EMAIL] reaches a person, and the remedy the Consumer Protection Act gives you remains yours.

Cancelling before delivery

An order can be cancelled from the order page until it is dispatched. After dispatch it becomes a return.
